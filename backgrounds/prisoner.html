Prisoner

Your life was measured by stone walls and the oppressive weight of chains. Perhaps you were wrongfully accused or served a sentence for your crimes. You survived by your wits, bartering secrets, and enduring hardship. Now that you are free, your cautious mind is always searching for weaknesses and calculating escape routes.

Skill Proficiencies: Choose two from Deception, Insight, Intimidation, Sleight of Hand.

Other Proficiencies: You can speak Thieves' Cant and are proficient in one type of gaming set.

Equipment: A set of common clothes, a pair of chained shackles, a shiv (improvised knife), a gaming set (dice or cards), and a belt pouch with 10 gp.

Talent: Choose one from Aware, Hand to Hand, or Mental Fortitude.
