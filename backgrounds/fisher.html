Fisher

Your life on the water spent in patient observation has forged a quiet resilience and sharpened your instincts. Perhaps you were a humble angler feeding a small village or a hardy deep-sea fisherman braving legendary storms. Beneath your quiet exterior, your keen mind is always watching and waiting for the right moment to strike.

Skill Proficiencies: Choose two from Athletics, Nature, Perception, and Survival.

Other Proficiencies: Water Vehicles and one type of artisan's tools or gaming set of your choice.

Equipment: A fishing rod with tackle, a skinning knife, traveler's clothes, a woven basket, and a belt pouch with 10 gp.

Talent: Choose one from Aware, Physical Fortitude, or Trade Skills.
