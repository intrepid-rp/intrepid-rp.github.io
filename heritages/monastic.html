Monastic Heritage

Monasteries demand a life stripped down to the essentials. Growing up in these secluded cloisters means long hours spent in quiet discipline, physical conditioning, and the preservation of old knowledge. You learned early on to trust your instincts, master your emotions, and respect the weight of tradition.

Stillness of Mind. You have advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened.

Monastic Lore. You gain proficiency in either History or Religion. You have advantage on checks to identify relics, decipher ancient texts, or recall sacred traditions.

Languages. You know Common and two additional languages, typically Elvish, Celestial, or Draconic.
