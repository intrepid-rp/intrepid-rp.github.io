Mountain Heritage

Mountain heritage characters are raised among the rugged peaks and windswept crags of high-altitude clans. Life above the treeline demands self-reliance, endurance, and an unwavering trust in one's companions. From an early age, these individuals are taught to navigate treacherous terrain, read unpredictable alpine weather, and utilize the vertical landscape to their advantage.

Sure-Footed. You have a climbing speed equal to your walking speed, and you have advantage on saving throws against being knocked prone.

Survivalist. You gain proficiency in the Athletics or Survival skill. Additionally, you are naturally adapted to cold climates and high altitudes.

Languages. You know Common and one additional language, typically Draconic, Dwarvish, or Giant.
