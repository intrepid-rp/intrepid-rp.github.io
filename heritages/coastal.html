Coastal Heritage

Life along the rugged cliffs, bustling ports, and sandy shores of the coast revolves around the ever-shifting tides and the boundless horizon. Whether you are a seasoned sailor, a net-caster, or a coastal trader, the sea dictates the rhythm of your community. You value adaptability and the bonds of a tight-knit crew.

Surfsense. You gain a swimming speed equal to your walking speed, and proficiency with water vehicles.

Seafarer's Lore. You gain proficiency in the Nature skill. You have advantage on checks related to predicting weather patterns, reading the tides, and identifying coastal hazards or marine life.

Languages. You know Common and two other languages. Coastal regions are one of the few places on land where Aqualan is spoken.
